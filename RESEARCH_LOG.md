**16:50 Pilot (1 seed, LightCNN, LOCO, no AMP; not used for reported results).** fixed_max 0.915 (AdaBN 0.924);
track_nf6 0.888; track_max 0.895; generic augmentation 0.917; kinematic augmentation 0.902.
→ **Original hypothesis H2 (tracked + noise-referenced front-end beats reference processing) NOT supported in the pilot.**
Errors on care sites are mostly pick ↔ drink. The cross-cohort Doppler extent differs (drink ≈0.7 m/s lab vs ≈1.8 m/s West Cumbria).
Late fusion fixed_max + track_nf6 = 0.925 (AdaBN 0.933): the two references look complementary.
→ New candidate: **dual-reference input** (max- and noise-referenced channels). Must be tested against seed-ensemble controls.

**17:03 Main queue started** (21 LightCNN conditions × 5 seeds, AMP), plus the classical feature queue on CPU.

**17:30–18:00 First 5-seed results (LOCO).** LightCNN fixed_max 0.918 ± 0.005 (AdaBN 0.920); dual-reference
0.914 (AdaBN 0.909) → **the dual-reference hypothesis is refuted** (Δ = −0.004, CI [−0.012, 0.006]). The pilot
late-fusion gain was seed noise: a plain 2-seed ensemble of fixed_max reaches 0.927. ResNet-18 0.898 (significantly
worse than LightCNN, Holm p = 0.03). SVM 0.818, RF 0.832; on the noise-referenced image SVM/RF collapse to 0.54/0.57.
SVM protocol inflation: random 0.860 → subject 0.850 → LOCO 0.818.
Lab vs care-site LOCO F1 (LightCNN): 0.941 vs 0.860.

**18:05 Site enrollment.** The pre-specified recipe (full FT, lr 3e-4) degraded F1 in a 1-seed smoke test, and so did
the source-person control. **Recipe choice is now nested** (pseudo-target = one training campaign). All recipes are
logged in the outer fold as post-hoc sensitivity. Running 5 seeds.

**19:45 Enrollment (5 seeds, nested recipe selection) finished.** k=1/2/3 enrolled persons: 0.898/0.913/0.915 vs
unadapted 0.913 (all Holm p = 1.0); AdaBN 0.919; care sites not improved. Source-person control is no worse.
→ The residual gap is person/cohort-level, not site-level.
LightCNN protocols: random 0.943, subject 0.931, LOCO 0.918 (lab 0.941 / care 0.860). ResNet-18 dual 0.911 vs 0.898.

**19:58 BUG FOUND AND FIXED (evaluation):** AdaBN (and the new TENT baseline) iterated over test samples in storage
order, which is sorted by activity class. Class-pure batches biased the averaged BN variances, and per-batch statistics
in TENT gave F1 0.76. Fix: shuffled batches with a fixed seed; TENT re-estimates population statistics before prediction.
Verified: TENT at lr 0 equals AdaBN (0.947 on the test fold). Regression test added (6 tests pass). All completed runs with
AdaBN numbers were moved to `results/runs_superseded/` and are re-run. SVM/RF are unaffected (no BN).
Source-model ("src") numbers were unaffected by the bug. The re-runs double as a reproducibility check.
Added baselines after self-review: TENT (label-free) and an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18.
Remote bundle: `remote_bundle.tgz` (210 MB; code + preprocessed images + manifests + weights; 4 worker queues `experiments/configs/remote_w*.txt`).
